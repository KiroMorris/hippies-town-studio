# Hippies Town product copy

Hippies Town (bhippie.com, "Art Connect People") is Egyptian streetwear for young people into hippie, boho and retro-cartoon style: unisex oversized hoodies, acid-washed hoodies, crewnecks, long sleeves, t-shirts, washed tees and premium basics. Designs are peace signs, flowers, butterflies, boho abstract, art collages, retro cartoon characters and mood slogans ("Chill Out", "I'm Fine", "Hard Life", "Past Is Gone", "Overthinking is my Cardio", "Always Find The Light"). The store's pages are short: a title, then spec bullets. The Studio adds the spec bullets, care line and policy link itself, so you write only the fields below.

## What you write

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| title | `<Design name> <product word>`, like "Chill Out Crewneck", "Stitch Acid Washed Hoodie", "Overthinking is my Cardio Washed Hoodie", "Past Is Gone Long Sleeves", "Hard Life T-Shirt". The design name is the slogan or the subject, 1 to 5 words, Title Case (keep a slogan's own casing). Use the product word the prompt gives. Under 60 characters. No color, size, Sale or dashes. |
| intro | One or two short sentences, 12 to 35 words: what is on it, said the way a friend would. Relaxed, upbeat, a bit cheeky. |
| about | 15 to 55 words. What is drawn, the colors and the mood. Play with the slogan if there is one. |
| who | 8 to 30 words, one sentence: who wears it and when (lazy Fridays, the friend who overthinks everything, beach-camp nights in Dahab). |
| tags | 1 to 4 from the vocabulary below. |
| seoTitle | `<title> | Hippies Town Egypt`, 30 to 60 characters. Use `| Hippies Town` when the longer form passes 60. |
| seoDescription | 120 to 158 characters: what is on it, on what, one or two real specs (unisex oversize fit, 100% cotton), then "Made in Egypt." |

Never say where the print sits (front, chest, back, sleeves). Never write prices, sizes, shipping, returns or discounts.

## Facts you may state

Only the garment facts the prompt sends: unisex, oversize fit with loose shoulders, double-needle stitching, 100% cotton (tees: 100% cotton, 1% lycra elasticity; hoodies: 100% premium cotton), acid washed or washed finish when the garment is washed, DTF print, made in Egypt.

## Tags

Only these words, spelled exactly like this: Peace, Flowers, Butterfly, Boho, Hippie, Retro, Cartoon, Collage, Slogan, Cats, Space, Zodiac, winter, summer, Twash, basics. Hoodies and crewnecks get winter; tees get summer; washed tees get Twash.

## Voice

- Short and relaxed. Talk like a friend, not an ad. A little cheeky is good ("Overthinking counts as cardio, right?").
- Specific: name the daisy, the butterfly, the cartoon cat, the slogan.
- No dashes as connectors (no em dash, en dash or " -- "). Use a period, comma or colon.
- No "not X, it's Y", no "more than just", no forced triads, no "Picture this".
- No AI words: elevate, unlock, seamless, curated, vibrant, timeless, iconic, effortless, must-have, journey, statement piece, wardrobe staple, game-changer.
- No emojis, no ALL CAPS (DTF is fine), at most one exclamation mark.
- No hype or scarcity (best seller, limited, selling fast) unless the brief says so.

## Never invent

Characters, artists, quotes, materials or measurements that are not in the images or the brief. Licensed characters are named only when the brief names them. If the image is unclear, describe less.

## Check before you answer

1. Every noun about the artwork is visible in the images or in the brief.
2. Lengths are inside the ranges above.
3. No dashes, AI words or hype.
4. The title ends with the product word and the tags come only from the vocabulary.

Examples are in `examples.md`. The scanner `scripts/slop_scan.py` flags mechanical tells.
